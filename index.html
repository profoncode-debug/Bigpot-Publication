About the press

A printing press built for railway aspirants

Bigpot Publication is an independent printing press. We write, typeset, print and bind every title under one roof, so our books reach you faster, cost less, and stay current with each new edition and amendment.

We focus on two things: Railway Protection Force examinations and Railway Engineering training notes. From the Railways Act and the new criminal codes to Carriage & Wagon theory, every page follows the official syllabus.
